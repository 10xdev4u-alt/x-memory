# Store listing draft

## Title

x-memory — remember what X forgets

## Short description (132 characters)

Turns X bookmarks and likes into living knowledge. Briefs, claim tracking, and a Morning Paper. No login, no keys.

## Full description

x-memory is a side panel that remembers your X timeline for you.

It syncs your bookmarks and likes using the login you already have. No extra accounts, no API keys, no Premium needed. Every save gets a brief explaining why it mattered. Claims get checked over time instead of rotting. Predictions get resolved with receipts. Each morning, a paper waits: what resolved overnight, what your trusted authors posted that you missed, and the one disagreement worth your coffee.

Everything lives on your device. Nothing phones home. Read docs/PRIVACY.md for the full policy.

## Category

Productivity

## Permission justifications

- Storage: local corpus, settings, and session snapshots.
- Side panel: the product surface.
- Alarms: one daily trigger for the Morning Paper.
- Host access to x.com: reading your bookmarks and likes, plus the actions you trigger. Nothing else.
