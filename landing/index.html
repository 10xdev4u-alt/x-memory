    Saves stop rotting

    Every bookmark gets a brief that says why it mattered. Claims get rechecked. Predictions get resolved with receipts. The graveyard becomes a garden.

    No login, no keys, no Premium

    Install, open the panel, sync. The extension uses your active X session and your account's Grok access. Folders stay a Premium feature elsewhere. Here, organization is automatic.

    A paper, not a feed

    Overnight, the corpus gets read against the last day on X. You wake up to what resolved, what you missed from trusted authors, and one disagreement worth your coffee. Listen to it on the way in.

    Yours, on your machine

    Corpus, briefs, and settings live in your browser. Nothing phones home. Diagnostics stay off unless you flip one toggle. Read the full policy in the repository.
